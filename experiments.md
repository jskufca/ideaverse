# Experimental Framework & Model Overview

## Overview of Study Types
To rigorously evaluate the model, the experimental campaign is divided into two distinct methodological approaches based on the guiding scientific question:
1. **Hypothesis-Testing Experiments**: Triggered by **binary/causal questions** ("Does $A$ cause $B$?"). These validate or invalidate specific directional predictions.
2. **Parametric & Sensitivity Studies**: Triggered by **characterization questions** ("How does $B$ scale across the range of $A$?"). These map continuous operational bounds.
