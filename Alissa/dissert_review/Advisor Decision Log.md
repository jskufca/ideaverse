# Advisor Decision Log

## Review principles

- Preserve the student’s mathematical contribution and voice.
- Distinguish substantive revisions from local copyedits.
- Make direct advisor edits when the correction is clear and does not alter the student’s argument.
- Return work to the student when a revision requires new explanation, mathematical justification, analysis, interpretation, or a choice the student should own.
- Frame model results as conditional on model assumptions unless empirical validation supports a stronger claim.

## Decision categories

- `advisor_edit` — I will make the change directly in Overleaf.
- `student_revision` — The student must revise and return the text.
- `discuss_with_student` — The concern is substantive but needs clarification before assigning a revision.
- `no_action` — I considered the issue and decided no change is needed.
- `resolved` — The revision has been completed and checked.
