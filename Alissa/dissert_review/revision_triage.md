# Revision Triage

## Purpose

Convert the full advisor decision log into a concise revision plan.

The student receives only substantive items requiring her mathematical,
methodological, or interpretive ownership. The advisor handles local edits,
terminology standardization, figure captions, cross-references, and final prose
polish.

### SR-04 — Reinterpret spatial experiments and conclusions

**Related decisions:** 017, 018, 044

Revise the size and multiple-zone experiments so that they:

- Describe comparisons of modeled configurations, not universal interventions
- Acknowledge changing city composition as greened area grows

- Do not claim that small zones benefit real cities or that several zones are at least as beneficial as one large zone
- State that the results do not strongly favor one of the tested configurations
